This is a simple record of a plan: to sit at Isha's programme, Soak in Ecstasy of Enlightenment with Sadhguru, in Jaipur on Sunday, 10 January 2027. Some things are already settled. Some are still open. Looking at each of them as they are is enough for now, and the travelling can be done with rest and gratitude.

The event

Isha's Soak in Ecstasy of Enlightenment with Sadhguru is on Sunday, 10 January 2027, at the Jaipur Exhibition & Convention Centre (JECC), Sitapura, Jaipur.

The day itself

Check-in closes at 8:00 AM. Be seated 20 minutes before the 9:00 AM first session.

Seating is on the floor, with a few chairs for medical needs.

Continue Inner Engineering practice (Shambhavi) beforehand.

The e-pass will come by email later.

Registration is already paid: ₹2,250.

Stay

The hotel is booked.

7 Apple Hotel

Tonk Road, Jaipur. It is 2.7 km from JECC, about 5 minutes away.

  • Check-in Saturday, 9 January 2027; check-out Monday, 11 January 2027 (2 nights).
  • Deluxe Queen room with breakfast: ₹9,389.52, paid at the hotel.
  • Hotel phone: +91 73750 70777.
  • Website: 7applehotels.com.

On Booking.com it is rated 8.1 from 82 reviews: comfort and location 8.8, staff 8.6, cleanliness 8.5. Guests praise the cleanliness, staff and food. The main complaint is small rooms.

Alternative considered

Cygnett Inn Airport, ₹8,800 with breakfast, rated 7.9 from 22 reviews. It is prepaid only, with complaints about the AC and the beds.

Three-day rhythm

Rest comes first: a quiet arrival, a full night before the event, and a daytime journey home.

9 January Leave mid-morning after the fog lifts, arrive by evening, have a light dinner, and sleep early.

10 January The event. The hotel is 5 minutes from JECC.

11 January Breakfast, then a daytime return. No pre-dawn start.

The point is good rest before the event, and a fresh body and mind for the ride home. January mornings in this belt are often 4 to 9°C, with a risk of dense fog on NH-44 from Karnal to Panipat.

Routes

By car the road is about 405 km, 4.5 to 5 hours, via the Trans-Haryana Expressway (NH-152D), then Narnaul and NH-48.

Bikes are not allowed on that expressway, so they go via NH-44 and NH-48 with the Gurugram service roads, or via Rohtak and Rewari: 420 to 436 km.

  • Car tolls are ₹580 to ₹900 each way. Bikes pay no tolls.
  • Petrol on 16 November: ₹102.75/L in Kurukshetra, ₹112.69/L in Jaipur. Fill up in Haryana.

Vehicles

Hyundai Creta SX petrol

A 50 L tank, 17 to 18 kmpl, and a safe range of about 720 to 765 km. No stop going. The round trip uses 46 to 52 L (₹4,700 to ₹5,300), with one top-up on the return.

Honda CBR650R

A 15.4 L tank (the low-fuel warning comes on at 3 L), 18 to 20 kmpl on the highway, and a range of 225 to 250 km. One stop each way, at about 200 to 220 km. The round trip uses 43 to 50 L (₹4,400 to ₹5,100).

Hero XPulse 210

A 13 L tank plus a 5 L jerrycan (Rotopax style), so 18 L in all. 30 to 35 kmpl, and a range of 480 to 560 km. One stop by choice. The round trip uses 25 to 30 L (₹2,500 to ₹3,100).

Jerrycan safety: keep it sealed, mounted away from the exhaust, filled to 90%, and re-check the bolts.

Bike rhythm

Stretch every hour. A hydration and susu break every 2 hours. About 3 or 4 breaks each way, 7 to 8 hours door to door.
